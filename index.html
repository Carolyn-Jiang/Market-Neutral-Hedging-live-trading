04 / TEST PROTOCOL

How the rounds work

1. Release and decide

Phrase I contains 12 data releases on 14–15 October. Phrase II adds 29 releases (rounds 13–41) on 19–23 October: 09:30, 10:30, 11:30, 12:30, 14:30, and 15:30 Monday–Thursday; Friday ends with the 14:30 release.

The first Phrase II news window is 16 October 16:00 to 19 October 09:30. Later windows start at the previous data release, including overnight windows. Action deadlines are five minutes before the next execution node.

2. Settle consistently

Each action enters at the next valuation node and exits at the following valuation node. For example, an action based on the 09:30 release enters at 10:30 and exits at 11:30. Missing valid actions are scored as F. Compound completed-round returns.

Phrase I cumulative returns carry forward into Phrase II without a reset. Teams may join later; rounds before their first valid submission remain flat. Rankings and risk metrics use all completed rounds.

3. Keep decisions private

FinMem and Moira each select a fresh initial stock pair from the first published Phrase II observation on 19 October at 09:30. FinMem locks its pair for all 29 Phrase II rounds. Moira immediately selects a different eligible pair if its relationship monitor reports BREAK.

Codex, FinMem, and Moira use only information available for the relevant round. Their decisions are recorded privately and scored locally with the same entry, exit, and return rules as every team. Team and baseline stock selections, actions, and submission times are not disclosed on this website. Only aggregate performance is public.

4. Finish Phrase II

The last Phrase II data release is on 23 October at 14:30, with its action due at 15:25. On 23 October, 15:30 is an entry/exit valuation boundary, not a scheduled website update. The 16:00 local job retrieves the exact 15:30 and 16:00 prices, settles both remaining intervals in sequence, and closes all positions. Neither boundary publishes another data batch.

Rankings, Sharpe ratios, maximum drawdowns, and return paths refresh after the local job verifies its settlements, including the final close. All times are in New York.

This is a competition simulation, not a brokerage execution service. Position sizing, valuation, execution timing, and transaction costs must be fixed in the verified scorer before trading opens.
